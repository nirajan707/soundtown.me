ABOUT SOUND TOWN

Music discovery
from around the world.

Sound Town is a global music discovery platform for songs, lyrics, music videos and artists. Visitors can search for a title, artist or remembered lyric, then explore official videos and full lyrics supplied for external artists.

What you can find

Sound Town brings together international music, Nepali releases, rap, covers, visualisers and selected music videos in one searchable place. Own-channel lyric releases feature an embedded YouTube video, while external artist pages preserve available written lyrics.

Official channels

Watch official Sound Town lyric releases on YouTube and follow Sound Town on TikTok.

Corrections and content removal

To report a correction, request lyric removal or suggest an official release, contact Sound Town through the official YouTube channel.
