Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Sound Town (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates soundtown.me. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the site and how it is used.

Information we collect

Sound Town does not require an account and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, or other personal information to browse the song library. Search terms are used in your browser to find songs and are not sent to a Sound Town server by the site.

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and request time, to deliver and secure the site.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, and personalize ads as described in their own policies. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google’s partner-sites policy.

You can manage or limit cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect advertising preferences but does not prevent you from using the song library.

Third-party services

Song videos are embedded from YouTube or linked to YouTube. When you play an embedded video or follow a YouTube link, YouTube may collect information according to its privacy policy. We also use Google Fonts to load the site’s typography.

How information is used

Information handled by the site or its service providers may be used to operate, secure, maintain, and improve Sound Town, deliver content, and provide advertising. We do not sell personal information directly.

Children’s privacy

Sound Town is a general-audience music site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through our YouTube channel.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the latest version was published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Sound Town through the Sound Town YouTube channel.
